Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

BMB Studio (“Movie Box,” “we,” “us”), a company registered in the UK, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Movie Box and how we use it.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. We may collect basic account details you choose to provide (such as an email for your bookings), as well as anonymous usage and diagnostic data that helps us keep the app fast and reliable.

How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the app. This includes showing showtimes near you, processing and delivering your ticket bookings, fixing bugs, and understanding which features are useful. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party data

Movie Box uses data from The Movie Database (TMDB) to display movie details such as titles, descriptions, posters, ratings, and cast information. This data is provided by TMDB and is subject to their own terms of use and privacy policy. Movie Box does not share your personal information with TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Advertising & tracking

Movie Box contains no advertising. We do not display ads, and we do not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers, and we do not use your data for ad targeting.

Your choices

You can use most of Movie Box without an account. You may request deletion of any account data at any time by contacting us.
